Our Globe

The living Earth, in your room — for Apple Vision Pro.

Open Our Globe and the Earth is there, in true color, lit by the sun exactly as it is right now: day where it's day, night where it's night, with the cities glowing. It arrives facing home and then turns slowly, west to east, like the real Earth.

Using it

  • Turn it: pinch and drag the globe, or reach out and touch it. Flick it to send it spinning; left alone, it goes back to its slow turn.
  • Make it bigger or smaller: look at a lower corner of the volume and drag the handle that appears. The globe fills whatever size you give it.
  • Move it: pinch and drag the bar under the volume.
  • Close it: the button next to that bar.

Questions

Is the light real? Yes. Our Globe works out where the sun is from your Vision Pro's clock, so the line between day and night is where it is on Earth right now. It moves on as the hours pass.

Why does it face where it does? It arrives facing the city of the time zone your Vision Pro is set to. No location access is needed.

Does it need the internet? No. The globe and its light are worked out on your Vision Pro. (When it's online, it counts its sessions anonymously: see the privacy policy.)

Something's not right?

Write to globe@lukaslau.com and tell us what you saw. Screenshots help.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no tracking, no personal data; sessions are counted anonymously: privacy policy.
