Our Globe — Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026

Our Globe shows the Earth, lit by the sun as it is right now, in a volume on your Apple Vision Pro. It does that entirely on your device, and it collects no data at all.

What the app uses

To work out where the sun is, Our Globe reads your Vision Pro's clock. To turn the globe toward home when it opens, it reads the time zone your Vision Pro is set to (for example Europe/Stockholm) and looks up that time zone's city in a list built into the app. Both stay on your device.

What the app doesn't do

Our Globe doesn't connect to the internet, doesn't ask for your location or any other permission, and has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. There is no server of ours that anything could be sent to.

Children

Our Globe collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a later version works differently (for example, if it adds features that need the internet), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
