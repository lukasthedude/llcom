Our Globe — Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026

Our Globe shows the Earth, lit by the sun as it is right now, in a volume on your Apple Vision Pro. It collects no personal data. To know how many people use it, it counts its sessions anonymously, as described below.

What the app counts

When you first open Our Globe, it makes up a random code for this installation: a string of letters and numbers that isn't connected to your name, your Apple Account, your device or anything else about you. Each time a session begins (the globe coming into view after at least half an hour away), the app sends that code to our database, which keeps three things about it: when it was first seen, when it was last seen, and how many sessions it has had. Nothing else: no location, no device details, nothing about what you look at.

We use these counts only to know how many people use Our Globe and how often. They aren't used for advertising or tracking, aren't combined with other data and aren't shared with anyone. The database is hosted by Supabase in the EU (Stockholm, Sweden). Like any server, it sees the internet address a request comes from, and Supabase may keep that in its logs for a short time; we don't store it. Deleting the app deletes its code: installed again, it makes a new one. Because a code can't be traced to anyone, we can't look up or delete a particular person's counts.

What stays on your device

To work out where the sun is, Our Globe reads your Vision Pro's clock. To turn the globe toward home when it opens, it reads the time zone your Vision Pro is set to (for example Europe/Stockholm) and looks up that time zone's city in a list built into the app. Neither leaves your device.

What the app doesn't do

Our Globe doesn't ask for your location or any other permission, and has no account, no advertising and no tracking. It works without an internet connection; sessions begun offline are counted the next time it's online.

Children

Our Globe collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a later version works differently, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
